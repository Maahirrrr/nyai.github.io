# Lawyer Discovery Skill

Provides city-aware advocate recommendations across Indian High Courts, District Courts, and specialized tribunals (NCLT, RERA, NCDRC, Family Courts).

## Supported Jurisdictions
- **Delhi / NCR**: Supreme Court, Delhi High Court, Patiala House, Saket, Tis Hazari
- **Mumbai / Maharashtra**: Bombay High Court, City Civil Court Fort, Sessions Court
- **Bengaluru / Karnataka**: Karnataka High Court, City Civil Court, Mayo Hall
- **Ahmedabad / Gujarat**: Gujarat High Court, City Civil Court Bhadra
- **Hyderabad / Telangana**: Telangana High Court, City Civil Court
- **Chennai / Tamil Nadu**: Madras High Court, George Town Court
- **Kolkata / West Bengal**: Calcutta High Court, Bankshall Court

## Verified Fields Included
- Full Advocate / Chamber Name
- State Bar Council Enrollment Number & Years of Experience
- Admitted Courts of Practice
- Specialized Practice Domains
- Physical Chamber Address
- Direct Phone and Email Contacts
- Key Investigative Questions to ask during first consultation

## Compliance
All advocate listings adhere strictly to **Bar Council of India Rule 36** as factual, non-solicitation, public registry information.
