What is the statutory difference between BNS 103 and BNS 101 regarding premeditation?
⚖
BNS § 103 (Old IPC 302) requires intentional killing without provocation, punishable with death or life imprisonment. BNS § 101 (Old IPC 304) applies where death occurs in a sudden mutual quarrel without premeditation (Section 100 Exceptions).
